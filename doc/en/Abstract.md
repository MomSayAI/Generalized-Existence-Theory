**Abstract**

This paper presents Generalized Existence Theory (GET), which seeks to address the most fundamental questions in philosophy and physics: **how does existence exist, and how does it evolve?**


### Methodological Foundation

Taking five empirically validated cornerstones as its sole inputs and grounded in the meta-philosophical principle that "rule execution is phenomenon; without process, nothing exists," GET identifies the sole dynamical ontology as the self-referential recursive execution of a protocol set \(L\).

Every speakable form \(M\) is the stable manifestation of this recursion:

$$
\Omega^{(M)} = \Phi(L)
$$

**Key Insight**: Existence is not a static entity but the running of rules; form is not substance but the stable manifestation of protocol execution.


### A Unified Criterion for Existence and Evolution

The degree of existence is quantified by:

$$
\Psi_M(t) = \frac{E_{\rm solid}(M,t)}{\tau_{\rm solid}(L)} + \frac{E_{\rm active}(M,t)}{\tau_{\rm active}(L)} + \eta(L) \cdot e(t)
$$

The necessary and sufficient condition for continued existence is that **active energy compensates net outflow**:

$$
\frac{E_{\rm active}(M,t)}{\tau_{\rm active}(L)} + \eta(L) \cdot e(t) \geq 0
$$

This criterion simultaneously addresses:
- **How existence exists**: a system must maintain non-negative net flow; otherwise, it disintegrates immediately
- **How existence evolves**: complexity grows solely through geometric selection and the four-phase cycle; otherwise, it returns to nothing
