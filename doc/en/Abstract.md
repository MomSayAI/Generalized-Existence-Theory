Abstract
This paper presents Generalized Existence Theory (GET), a zero-ad-hoc framework that directly addresses the most fundamental questions in philosophy and physics: how does existence exist, and how does it evolve?
Taking five empirically validated cornerstones as its sole inputs and grounded in the meta-philosophical principle that “rule execution is phenomenon; without process, nothing exists,” GET identifies the sole dynamical ontology as the self-referential recursive execution of a protocol set $  L  $. Every speakable form $  M  $ is the stable manifestation of this recursion:
$$\Omega^{(M)} = \Phi(L).$$
Existence is quantified by a unified survival criterion:
$$\Psi_M(t) = \frac{E_{\rm solid}}{\tau_{\rm solid}} + \frac{E_{\rm active}}{\tau_{\rm active}} + \eta \cdot e(t),$$
with continued existence requiring that active energy compensates net outflow. This single criterion simultaneously answers how existence persists (non-negative net flow must be maintained) and how it evolves (complexity grows solely through geometric selection and the four-phase cycle).
Without free parameters, GET derives sharp, binary predictions: dark matter consists of frozen $  d=0  $ mid-solidification states with strictly zero non-gravitational cross-section; dark energy is a uniform frozen background with $  w_\Lambda \equiv -1  $ exactly and constant density; gravity is pure $  d=0  $ geometry requiring no quantization. Wave-particle duality and macroscopic multiple identities (e.g., the same person appearing as “father”, “friend”, or “doctor” to different observers) are unified as instances of the same protocol-resonance and selection mechanism.
GET establishes a rigorous four-level falsification system (Corollary 22) with binary conditions under which the theory fails entirely, with no adjustable parameters or retreat. First-time readers are invited to begin with Appendix E, recognizing through everyday experiences—hunger, fatigue, endurance—the existence grammar they have always spoken.
The central contribution of GET is to provide, without additional entities or ad hoc assumptions, a unified syntax that explains why and how existence exists and evolves, thereby bridging ontology, physics, and cognition under one coherent framework.
