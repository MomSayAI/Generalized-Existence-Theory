**Abstract**

This paper proposes Generalized Existence Theory (Generalized Existence Theory, GET), aiming to establish a unified syntax of existence spanning from vacuum fluctuations to intelligent civilization.

**Methodological Foundation.** GET strictly follows the serious scientific paradigm of Newton–Einstein–Popper: its inputs are restricted to five empirically validated cornerstones—the energy–time constraint and power-law statistics of quantum fluctuations, the three laws of thermodynamics, mass–energy equivalence, and the dependence of structured existence on rule sets. Grounded in the meta-philosophical stance that "rule execution is phenomenon; without process, nothing exists," GET posits that the only dynamical ontology is the self-referential recursive execution of a protocol set \(L\); form \(M\) is its stable external manifestation. This yields the unified syntax:

\[
\Omega^{(M)} = \Phi(L)
\]

—every speakable existence is an instance of its protocol set's generating function.

**A Unified Criterion for Existence and Evolution.** The degree of existence is quantified as:

\[
\Psi_M(t) = \frac{E_{\text{solid}}(M,t)}{\tau_{\text{solid}}(L)} + \frac{E_{\text{active}}(M,t)}{\tau_{\text{active}}(L)} + \eta(L) \cdot e(t)
\]

where \(E_{\text{solid}}\) and \(E_{\text{active}}\) are solidified-state and active-state energy respectively, \(e(t)\) is the instantaneous net flux, and \(\tau\) and \(\eta\) are species-specific parameters. The necessary and sufficient condition for continued existence is:

\[
\frac{E_{\text{active}}(M,t)}{\tau_{\text{active}}(L)} + \eta(L) \cdot e(t) \geq 0
\]

This survival criterion reveals the death mechanism directly: when active energy can no longer compensate for net outflow, the system disintegrates immediately—solidified energy \(E_{\text{solid}}\) cannot help in the moment of crisis.

**The Only Path to Complexity Growth.** Transition from recursion depth \(d\) to \(d+1\) occurs **only** through "geometric selection + the four-phase cycle (Trial—Capture—Solidify—Generate)." An individual gains upgrade eligibility only when its net borrowing energy curve \(E(t)\) intersects the threshold \(\epsilon_{\text{th}}^{(d+1)}\) and the interval between intersections satisfies \(\Delta t \geq N_{\text{min}}^{(d+1)}\). This principle is a logical necessity derived from the energy–time constraint and power-law statistics, not an independent assumption.

**The Unifying Power of Axiom III.** GET reveals that the Heisenberg uncertainty principle \(\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \geq \hbar/2\) is a special case of Axiom III's scaling constraint for the \(d=0\) species \(L_0\)—every stable existence must satisfy its species-specific energy–time threshold.

**Sharply Falsifiable Predictions.** Without free parameters, GET derives:

- **Dark matter** (Category B) is a frozen \(d=0\) excited state; its non-gravitational interaction cross section is **strictly zero**.
- **Dark energy** (Category C) is a uniformly frozen background; its equation of state \(w_\Lambda \equiv -1\) holds exactly, its density \(\rho_\Lambda\) is strictly constant (\(\dot{\rho}_\Lambda = 0\)), and its observed fraction increases solely due to the dilution of matter and radiation.
- **Gravity** is a pure geometric effect at \(d=0\), requiring no quantization.
- Energy transformation follows a strict serial path: matter \(\to\) radiation \(\to\) redshift freezing \(\to\) dark energy background.

**A Commitment to Falsifiability.** This paper establishes a four-level falsification system (Corollary 22) with binary adjudication criteria. Any person or institution may participate—using AGI, including brute-force enumeration. If any falsification condition is met, GET fails; if none is met, the assertions remain valid under the same adjudication protocol.
