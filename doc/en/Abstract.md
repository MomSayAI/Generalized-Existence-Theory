**Abstract**

This paper presents Generalized Existence Theory (GET), which seeks to address the most fundamental questions in philosophy and physics: **how does existence exist, and how does it evolve?**

Taking five empirically validated cornerstones as its sole inputs and grounded in the meta-philosophical principle that "rule execution is phenomenon; without process, nothing exists," GET identifies the sole dynamical ontology as the self-referential recursive execution of a protocol set \(L\). Every speakable form \(M\) is the stable manifestation of this recursion:
\[
\Omega^{(M)} = \Phi(L).
\]

The degree of existence is quantified by a unified survival criterion:
\[
\Psi_M(t) = \frac{E_{\rm solid}}{\tau_{\rm solid}} + \frac{E_{\rm active}}{\tau_{\rm active}} + \eta \cdot e(t),
\]
with continued existence requiring that active energy compensates net outflow. This criterion simultaneously addresses "how existence exists" (a system must maintain non-negative net flow) and "how existence evolves" (complexity grows solely through geometric selection and the four-phase cycle).

GET thereby interprets dark matter as frozen mid-solidification states at \(d=0\) with strictly zero non-gravitational cross-section, dark energy as a uniform frozen background with \(w_\Lambda \equiv -1\) exactly and constant density, and gravity as pure \(d=0\) geometry. It further unifies wave-particle duality and macroscopic multiple identities as instances of the same protocol-resonance mechanism.

GET establishes a rigorous four-level falsification system (Corollary 22) with binary conditions. First-time readers are invited to begin with Appendix E, recognizing through everyday experiences (hunger, fatigue, endurance) the existence grammar they have always spoken.

The central contribution of GET is to provide — without additional entities or ad hoc assumptions — a unified syntax that explains why and how existence exists and evolves, thereby bridging ontology, physics, and cognition under one coherent framework.
